DateTimePlugin Time

Shows the current date and time for any time zone.

Display mode can be configured to show date, time, or both.

Supports 12-hour or 24-hour format.

Time zone can be specified or uses the one from settings.

First day of the week is configurable.

Format uses strftime() syntax.

Format Specifiers
  • %b - Abbreviated month name (3 chars)
  • %d - Day of the month (01-31)
  • %m - Month (01-12)
  • %H - Hour 24h (00-23)
  • %I - Hour 12h (01-12)
  • %M - Minute (00-59)
  • %p - AM or PM
  • %Y - Year
Configuration
If empty, settings time zone applies.
